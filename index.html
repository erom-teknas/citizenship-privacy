🛡️ Zero Personal Data Collection Guarantee

Canadian Citizenship Test 2026 does NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal identifiable data, location data, device identifiers, or biometric information. Your exam progress and preferences remain strictly on your local device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe your study preparation is personal. The app operates with complete privacy:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account, sign in with email, or provide your name.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not track individual user behavior or install third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No Advertising IDs: We do not serve third-party ads and do not collect IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers).
  • No Audio Recording: Speech audio is synthesized via local edge narration. No microphone access is requested or used.

2. Local On-Device Storage

To deliver an uninterrupted study experience, the application stores the following information locally on your device via standard sandbox storage:

  • Exam progress, current question index, and answer history.
  • Active province or territory selection (e.g., Ontario, British Columbia).
  • Language preference (English or French).
  • Audio playback speed and sound effect toggle preferences.

This data never leaves your physical iPhone and is automatically deleted if you uninstall the application.

3. In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

Purchases for Pro Pass Lifetime Access are processed directly through Apple's secure In-App Purchase system (StoreKit). We do not receive, process, or store your credit card, billing address, or financial details. All transactions are governed by Apple's Media Services Terms and Conditions.

4. Restoration of Purchases

In full compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 3.1.1, you can restore previous purchases on any supported iOS device signed into your Apple ID by tapping the "Restore Purchases" button inside the app settings or paywall screen.

5. Official Syllabus Disclaimer

This application is an independent educational tool designed to help applicants prepare for the Canadian Citizenship Knowledge Test administered by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). It is based on the official study guide Discover Canada: The Rights and Responsibilities of Citizenship. This application is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of the Government of Canada or IRCC.
